How to play

  1. Move bright worms through free cells by pushing the head or tail one tile at a time.
  2. Match both ends of a worm to a socket pair with the same required length.
  3. Pulsing worms are merge-ready. Select one and press Merge to fuse it with its nearest matching partner.
  4. When enough holes are closed, press Resolve to seal pairs, clear those sockets, and spawn new worms.
  5. If a mandatory merge exists after a resolve, the next pipe batch waits until you merge once.
