Snake Merge Defense

Collect colored orbs with both active ends, line up attack rows at the wall, and clear matching blocks before any lane reaches the top.

Mobile layout keeps setup compact and focused on quick portrait play.
Desktop layout shows a quick visual tutorial on the right.

Move and collect orbs
Drag across the grid. The active end steps cell by cell and collects colored orbs on contact.
Control both ends
You can drag either the head or the tail. The closer end to the pointer becomes the active one.
Shoot from attack rows
Segments parked in the attack rows fire upward automatically when their color matches a valid target.
